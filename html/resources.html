GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is financial crime?

Financial crime involves illegal activities carried out for financial gain, including fraud, money laundering and corruption.

How can I report suspicious activities?

You can use the Submit Petition section of our website to send information for review.

What is cybercrime?

Cybercrime refers to illegal activities involving computers, digital devices or the internet.

Why is financial crime prevention important?

It helps protect individuals, businesses and the economy from financial losses.

How can I view available auction properties?

Visit the Available Lands for Auction section to view listed properties and their details.
