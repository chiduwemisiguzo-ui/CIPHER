OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE

Agency Issues Public Advisory on Financial Fraud

15 November 2026 NBFI Press Office

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

The National Bureau of Financial Investigation (NBFI) wishes to bring to the attention of members of the public the increasing importance of financial awareness and responsible financial practices.

The agency encourages individuals, businesses and financial institutions to remain vigilant when engaging in financial transactions. Members of the public are advised to verify the authenticity of financial offers, business proposals and investment opportunities before making commitments.

Public Advisory

The public is encouraged to take the following precautions:

  • Verify the identity of individuals and organisations before making financial transactions.
  • Avoid sharing confidential banking information with unverified individuals.
  • Report suspicious financial activities through appropriate official channels.
  • Remain cautious of investment opportunities that promise unrealistic returns.

Commitment to Public Protection

NBFI remains committed to promoting transparency, accountability and public awareness. The agency will continue to work towards addressing financial irregularities and protecting the interests of the public.

Members of the public are encouraged to obtain information from verified agency communication channels and avoid spreading unconfirmed reports.
